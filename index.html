About the Lake

Lake Apo is a crater lake in Barangay Guinoyoran, Valencia City. It was once recognized as the cleanest inland body of water in Northern Mindanao. In Bisaya, apo means elder or grandparent.

Visitor Guidelines

  • Do not litter. Take your trash with you.
  • Follow the staff’s instructions at all times.
  • Respect the lake, wildlife, and local community.

Safety Reminders

  • Swim only where and when it is allowed.
  • Always supervise children near the water.
  • Wear a life vest when using rafts or boats.

Getting There

Lake Apo is about 11 km from the Valencia City proper. From Cagayan de Oro or Davao, travel to Valencia City first, then continue to Barangay Guinoyoran.
