Learn how volcanic forces formed Bukidnon's cleanest inland crater lake and how local communities protect it.

Volcanic Origins & Meaning

A Crater Lake in the Highlands

Nestled at 640 meters above sea level in Barangay Guinoyoran, Lake Apo was formed inside a volcanic depression surrounded by lush hills. Its basin collects fresh rainwater, keeping the water calm and clear.

Meaning of "Apo"

In Bisaya, apo means elder or grandparent—a fitting name for a peaceful highland landmark valued by generations of locals and visitors.

Conservation & Ecosystem

Lake Apo once earned recognition as the cleanest inland body of water in Northern Mindanao.
